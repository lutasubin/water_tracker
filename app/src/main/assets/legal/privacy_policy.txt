WE App INC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Water Tracker and Weight Log mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use
We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). Any data you enter into the app, such as weight, height, and water intake, is used solely for providing the app's functionality (BMI calculation and progress tracking).

2. 100% Offline Storage (Local Data)
No Servers: We do not use external servers or cloud storage (No Firebase, No AWS).

Local Storage: All your health data is stored locally on your device's internal memory.

Data Control: You have full control over your data. You can edit or delete it anytime by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

3. No Third-Party Services
No Ads: This version does not contain advertisements (No AdMob).

No Analytics: We do not use tracking tools like Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics.

No Data Sharing: We do not sell or share any user information with third parties.

4. GDPR Compliance (Europe)
For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we do not collect data on servers, your "Right to be Forgotten" and "Right to Access" are managed by you directly on your device.

5. CCPA/CPRA Compliance (California, USA)
We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act. We do not collect any identifiers for commercial purposes.

6. COPPA Compliance (Children's Privacy)
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All data is processed locally on the user's device without our intervention.
